# Task 1: Conversation 1

## Scenario
Please imagine that you are a sedentary person seeking help from a coach to improve your physical activity.

## Your Task
1. Briefly explore each page of the app.
2. Navigate to the “Coach” page and select the assigned coach for Task 1, which was sent to your email prior to the meeting.
3. Talk with the coach based on your personal experience or any scenario you can imagine.
4. After completing the conversation, please fill out the short survey on the left side.
5. Please also complete the evaluation survey on the next page. You will be asked to enter an access code, which has been sent to your email before the meeting. 

## Instructions
- Take your time - there's no rush
- Try to say everything that comes to your mind while you engage with the task
- If you get stuck, that's okay - just describe what you're trying to do
- When you're done (or if you can't complete it), answer the questions on the right
- Click "Next" once you have finished questions

Click “Next” to complete the AI coach evaluation survey.
