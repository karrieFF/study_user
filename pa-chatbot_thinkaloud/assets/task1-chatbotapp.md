# Task 1: Conversation 1

## Scenario

Imagine that you are seeking help from a coach to become more physically active.

## Your Task

### 1. Open the audio recording 
1. Navigate to the **Profile** page.
2. Click the **Start Recording** button on the **Profile** page.

### 2. Explore the App

- Briefly explore each page of the app.

### 3. Interact with the Coach

1. Navigate to the **Coach** page and select the assigned coach for Task 1.
2. Talk with the coach based on your personal experience or any scenario you can imagine.
3. After completing the conversation, click the **End** button on the **Coach** page.
4. Navigate to **Profile** page, then click **End Recording**. 

### 4. Complete the Evaluation Survey

1. Fill out the short survey on the left side.
2. Complete the evaluation survey on the next page. You will be asked to enter an access code, which was sent to your email before the meeting.

## Instructions

- Take your time — there's no rush.
- Try to say everything that comes to your mind while you engage with the task.
- If you get stuck, that's okay — just describe what you're trying to do.
- When you're done (or if you can't complete it), answer the questions on the left.

---

Please click **Next** to answer the AI coach evaluation survey.
