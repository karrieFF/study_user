# Welcome to the AI Health Coach Evaluation Study

Thank you for participating in this study! 

## Purpose
This study aims to evaluate the performance of AI health coach for promoting physical activity, including functionality, usability, user experience, etc.

## What to Expect
- This evaluation will take approximately **60-90 minutes**.
- You will briefly explore the **AI health coach app**.
- You will have conversations with **two AI health coaches**.
- You will answer some follow-up questions and fill a survey after each conversation.
- We will audio-record the entire testing session.

## Privacy
- Your participation is voluntary.
- You can withdraw at any time.
- Video and audio recordings will be used only for research purposes.
- All data will be anonymized and kept confidential.

By continuing, you consent to participate in this study.
