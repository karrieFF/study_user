# Getting Started: Expectations of a Health Coach

## Your Task
Imagine that you are physically inactive and would like to seek support from a health coach to become more physically active.

**What would you expect from the health coach?**

## Instructions
- Take your time. There is no time limit.
- Think aloud and share everything that comes to mind.
- There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your personal expectations.

Once you have shared your expectations, please click **Next** to set up the AI coach app.
