# Welcome to the AI Coach Evaluation Study

Thank you for participating in this study! 

## Purpose
This study aims to evaluate AI coach for promoting physical activity. Your feedback will help us improve the AI coach's functionality, usability, and user experience.

## What to Expect
- This study will take approximately 60-90 minutes.
- You will briefly explore the AI coach app.
- You will have conversations with the **two AI coaches**.
- You will answer some questions after the tasks.
- We will record your audio during the testing session.

## Privacy
- Your participation is voluntary.
- You can withdraw at any time.
- Video and audio recordings will be used only for research purposes.
- All data will be anonymized and kept confidential.

By continuing, you consent to participate in this study.
