# Think-Aloud Protocol Instructions

## What is Think-Aloud?

<div style="max-width: 100%; display: flex; gap: 14px; align-items: flex-start; background: #e7f5ff; border: 1px solid #a5d8ff; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px 20px; margin-bottom: 28px; font-size: 17px; line-height: 1.6;">
  <span style="font-size: 26px; line-height: 1.2;">🗣️</span>
  <div>During this study, we ask you to <strong>think aloud</strong>. This means speaking your thoughts as you interact with the AI coach.</div>
</div>

## How to Think Aloud

<div style="max-width: 100%; display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(300px, 1fr)); gap: 16px; margin-bottom: 28px; line-height: 1.55;">
  <div style="border: 1px solid #dee2e6; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; background: #f8f9fa;">
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 6px;">💬 Say everything that comes to mind</div>
    <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #495057;">Describe what you are doing, what you plan to do next, and any expectations, thoughts, or reactions you have, even if they seem unrelated to the task.</div>
  </div>
  <div style="border: 1px solid #dee2e6; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; background: #f8f9fa;">
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 6px;">✅ No right or wrong thoughts</div>
    <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #495057;">Any thoughts are okay; just be honest.</div>
  </div>
  <div style="border: 1px solid #dee2e6; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; background: #f8f9fa;">
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 6px;">🔁 Speak continuously</div>
    <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #495057;">Try to keep talking, even if your thoughts feel unstructured or uncertain.</div>
  </div>
  <div style="border: 1px solid #dee2e6; border-radius: 12px; padding: 16px; background: #f8f9fa;">
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 16px; margin-bottom: 6px;">🙂 Be natural</div>
    <div style="font-size: 15px; color: #495057;">Speak naturally, as if you are thinking aloud or talking to a friend.</div>
  </div>
</div>

## Helpful Reminders

<div style="max-width: 100%; background: #fff9db; border: 1px solid #ffe066; border-radius: 12px; padding: 18px 20px; margin-bottom: 28px; font-size: 15px; line-height: 1.55;">
  <div style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>🔇 Please avoid staying silent for long periods.</strong></div>
  <div style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>✨ You do not need to be perfect.</strong> Imagine you are alone and simply say whatever comes to mind.</div>
  <div style="margin-bottom: 12px;"><strong>🤷 You do not need to explain or justify your actions.</strong> Just think aloud. You do not need to justify what you are doing.</div>
  <div><strong>🎯 You do not need to plan.</strong> Please do not plan what to say. Just speak naturally as thoughts come up.</div>
</div>
