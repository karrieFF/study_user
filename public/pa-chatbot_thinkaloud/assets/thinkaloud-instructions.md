# Think-Aloud Protocol Instructions

## What is Think-Aloud?
During this study, we ask you to **think aloud**. This means speaking your thoughts as you interact with the AI coach.

## How to Think Aloud
- **Try to say everything that comes to mind while you work on the task.** For example, describe what you are doing, what you plan to do next, and any expectations, thoughts, or reactions you have, even if they seem unrelated to the task.
- **There are no right or wrong thoughts.** Any thoughts are okay; just be honest.
- **Speak continuously.** Try to keep talking, even if your thoughts feel unstructured or uncertain.
- **Be natural.** Speak naturally, as if you are thinking aloud or talking to a friend.

## Examples of Think-Aloud
- "Hmm... I guess I'd probably start by looking for the chatbot."
- "I'm looking for a place to start the conversation..."
- "I expected it to understand that, but it seems confused..."
- "Hmm, I'm not sure what this means..."
- "Oh, that's helpful! It gives me exactly what I needed."

## What NOT to Do
- **Please avoid staying silent for long periods.**
- **You do not need to be perfect** - Imagine you are alone and simply say whatever comes to mind.
- **You do not need to explain or justify your actions** - Just think aloud. You do not need to justify what you are doing.
- **You do not need to plan** - Please do not plan what to say. Just speak naturally as thoughts come up.
