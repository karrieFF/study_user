# Welcome to the AI Health Coach Follow-up Test

## Purpose
This follow-up test aims to evaluate the AI health coach’s ability to review your physical activity progress, provide personalized and nonjudgmental feedback, and tailor the coaching session to your experiences, motivation, and needs since the previous session.

## What to Expect
This follow-up test will take approximately **30 minutes** in total.

You will evaluate three things: the **Activity page** and **two AI health coaches**.

| Step | What you will do | Est. time |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Sign in to the app, wake up the coach, and start recording | ~3 min |
| Task 1 | View your Fitbit data on the **Activity page** and log an activity in your SMART goal | ~5 min |
| Task 2 | Talk with **{{coach1Short}}**, then complete a short survey | ~10 min |
| Task 3 | Talk with **{{coach2Short}}**, then complete a short survey | ~10 min |
| Wrap-up | Stop recording and finish | ~2 min |

You will record your think-aloud audio using the **Recording** function in the app.

## Privacy
- Your participation is voluntary.
- You can withdraw at any time.
- Audio recordings will be used only for research purposes.
- All data will be anonymized and kept confidential.

By continuing, you consent to participate in this study.
